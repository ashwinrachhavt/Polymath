# Market research: Routine

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Analyst:** overnight build session · **Status:** current as of 2026-10-25 sources
**Category:** All-in-one work OS (tasks + calendar + notes) with an agent layer.

## What it is

YC-backed "one app for tasks, calendar & notes, powered by AI": a universal
inbox over all your tools, bidirectional integrations, and - the 2.0 pitch -
an **AI workforce**: specialized background agents you delegate research,
triage, and follow-ups to, plus MCP/API so your own external assistant can
operate the workspace. 100k+ users claimed. [S1]

## Current feature set (verified 2026-10-25)

- **Universal inbox**: import tasks, messages, and notifications from every
  connected app into one triage stream. [S1]
- **Tasks, meetings, projects, notes** in one workspace, fast and
  keyboard-first (users cite speed vs. Notion setup). [S1]
- **AI delegation ("workforce")**: background agents for research, triage,
  follow-ups. [S1]
- **AI automations**: workflows that keep information up to date without
  manual effort. [S1]
- **MCP + API**: connect an external AI assistant to operate the workspace. [S1]
- Bidirectional integrations across the usual suite. [S1]

## Business model

Freemium with paid tiers (not re-verified tonight). YC-backed. [S1]

## Strengths

- **The fullest "agent layer over a work OS" articulation** in the set:
  delegation, automations, and MCP as first-class product, not bolt-ons.
- Speed + customizability reputation (the anti-Notion-setup complaint is
  their wedge).
- Universal inbox, again: the fourth sighting of intake-triage as the
  category's front door (Akiflow, Saner, Axiom's ASH-272).

## Weaknesses

- **Trust model is invisible**: what background agents may do, at what cost,
  with what review, is not on the page. "AI workforce" with no visible
  receipts is the category's confidence gap.
- Breadth over depth: notes knowledge-work (summaries, verification, links)
  reads thin next to specialist tools.
- Delegation to background agents is exactly where silent wrongness hurts.

## What Axiom can assimilate

1. **The delegation vocabulary**: "delegate research, triage, follow-ups"
   maps to Alfred's skills; Routine proves users accept delegating whole
   verbs, not just answering chats. The difference Axiom ships: each
   delegated action lands as a reviewable proposal/run row.
2. **MCP as a second sighting** (with Amie): the personal OS exposed to the
   user's chosen assistant is becoming a distribution expectation. Decision
   item for the owner (authz boundary), not an implementation task.
3. **Automations that "keep everything up to date"** - the daybook + outbox
   already do this mechanically; the assimilable part is the user-facing
   framing (named automations, visible runs).

## Where Axiom can differentiate

1. **Receipts for the workforce**: run rows, proposals with provenance,
   quota ledger - Routine's agents are a black box by comparison.
2. **Verified knowledge under the work**: Routine's notes are notes;
   Axiom's are proven claims.
3. **Owner-controlled automation**: nothing runs in the background without a
   registry entry and a budget.
