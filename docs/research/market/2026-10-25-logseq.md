# Market research: Logseq

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Analyst:** overnight build session · **Status:** current as of 2026-10-25 sources
**Category:** Local-first outliner PKM (open source).

## What it is

The open-source, privacy-first outliner: plain-text files on your disk,
journals-first daily notes, block references, and a query language over your
own graph. The community darling of the "own your data" wing of PKM, aimed
at students, academics, writers, PMs, and developers. [S1]

## Current feature set (verified 2026-10-25)

- **Journals-first capture**: the daily note is the front door. [S1]
- **Outliner model**: indentation as structure; block-level addressing and
  embedding. [S1]
- **Task management** inside the outliner (TODO states, deadlines). [S1]
- **Queries**: a real query language generating live tables over the graph. [S1]
- **PDF annotation** and **flashcards** built in. [S1]
- **Local files + data control** as the headline privacy posture. [S1]

## Business model

Free and open source; donations/ sponsorship. Sync as an optional paid
service (not re-verified tonight). [S1]

## Strengths

- Zero lock-in: markdown/org files, open code, offline by default.
- The query language turns notes into views without leaving the tool -
  power users build real workflows on it.
- Journals-first makes daily capture frictionless.

## Weaknesses

- **All manual**: no summarization, no classification, no assistant; the
  organization burden is the user's.
- The outliner forces one shape on every kind of knowledge; non-hierarchical
  thinking fights the tool.
- Performance and sync complaints are longstanding community themes.

## What Axiom can assimilate

1. **Journals-first capture**: the daybook already is this; Logseq validates
   the daily note as the default surface.
2. **A query language over the graph** as the power-user escape hatch -
   longer-term, but the "saved views" table-stakes pattern (Bases, smart
   queries, Smart Spaces) is the lighter version of the same idea.
3. **Flashcards from notes** - spaced repetition over verified zettels is a
   natural, additive artifact kind.

## Where Axiom can differentiate

1. **The model does the filing**: Logseq asks the user to structure; Axiom
   classifies, proposes, and links with receipts.
2. **Documents as first-class objects** (extraction, classification,
   obligations) vs. annotations on files.
3. **Action**: tasks and calendar are the substrate, not plugins.
