# Market research: Granola

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Analyst:** overnight build session · **Status:** current as of 2026-10-25 sources
**Category:** Botless AI meeting notepad.

## What it is

The meeting-notes tool that won the "no bot in the call" niche: it listens
to the computer's own audio, so nothing joins the meeting; notes are
private by default and shareable by choice. Notes, actions, and memory for
"back-to-back meetings." Second sighting (with Amie) of botless capture as
the meeting category's winning UX. [S1]

## Current feature set (verified 2026-10-25)

- **Botless capture from computer audio** - works with Zoom, Meet, Teams,
  "every other meeting app," and in-person. [S1]
- **Private by default**, explicit share. [S1]
- **Notes + actions + memory** from meetings; enhancement of the user's own
  rough notes. [S1]
- Cross-platform: macOS, Windows, iOS, Android - new: Apple Watch. [S1]

## Business model

Freemium with paid team tiers (not re-verified tonight). [S1]

## Strengths

- **The consent-friendly capture model**: no bot, no calendar permission
  wall, no social awkwardness - the reason it spreads bottom-up.
- "Enhance my rough notes" is a better human-in-the-loop shape than
  replace-the-note-taker: the user stays engaged, AI fills in.
- Memory across meetings is the retention hook.

## Weaknesses

- Meetings only; no knowledge graph, no tasks substrate, no reading.
- Transcription + summary trust is unverified generation, as ever.
- Single-player memory with sharing bolted on.

## What Axiom can assimilate

1. **Enhance-don't-replace**: for any capture surface, take the user's rough
   input and enhance it (structure, links, actions) rather than generating
   from nothing - the human stays the author, the AI is the editor. Applies
   to capture triage UX and to meeting artifacts.
2. **Botless audio capture** if/when Axiom records meetings: the consent
   ergonomics are the feature (matches the Amie finding).
3. **Watch/phone as capture surfaces** - voice capture into the inbox is
   already in scope; Granola's Watch app says the habit is real.

## Where Axiom can differentiate

1. **Meetings join the same verified graph** as reading and tasks (repeat of
   the Amie point - the pattern is now confirmed by two players).
2. **Actions with follow-through**: Granola lists actions; Axiom proposes
   them into the task system with provenance.
3. **Verified claims from transcripts** - the summarizer's quote-pinning
   applied to meeting text.
