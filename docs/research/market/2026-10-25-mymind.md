# Market research: mymind

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Analyst:** overnight build session · **Status:** current as of 2026-10-25 sources
**Category:** AI-organized visual capture ("remember everything, organize nothing").

## What it is

A private visual bookmark/note tool with a strong anti-organization thesis:
save anything in one click, and AI classifies what it IS (article, product,
book, recipe) and makes it findable - no folders, tags, or filing ever asked
of the user. Manifesto-grade privacy positioning: no social features, no
vanity metrics, no collaboration, no ads. [S1]

## Current feature set (verified 2026-10-25)

- **One-click save** (iOS, Android, Chrome/Edge/Safari extensions); AI
  recognizes the content type and presents it appropriately. [S1]
- **Associative search**: by color, keyword, brand, date - "whatever you think
  of first." [S1]
- **Smart bookmarking**: per-type treatment (articles vs. products vs. books
  vs. recipes). [S1]
- **Distraction-free reader** for saved articles. [S1]
- **Smart Spaces**: auto-sorting collections with user-defined terms. [S1]
- **Frictionless notes** with a Focus Mode for expanding later. [S1]

## Business model

Paid subscription (positioned as the anti-ad, anti-data product; price not
re-verified tonight). [S1]

## Strengths

- **The zero-organization promise, kept**: the strongest counter-position to
  folder/tag tools, and genuinely loved by visual thinkers.
- Type-aware presentation: a recipe does not render like an article.
- Privacy as identity, not a settings page.

## Weaknesses

- **Recall, not reasoning**: it finds what you saved; it does not summarize,
  connect claims, verify, or act.
- Single-player by design (no collaboration is a *feature*); no workflow,
  tasks, or agent.
- Opaque organization: when the AI misfiles, there is no structure to inspect
  or correct.

## What Axiom can assimilate

1. **Type-aware presentation of captures.** Mymind's "no URL is treated the
   same" is the UI half of Axiom's type classifier (ASH-272): once a capture
   is classified, render it as what it IS - a contact card, a task row, a
   reader card - not a generic note. The classification ladder produces the
   verdict; presentation should consume it.
2. **Associative recall axes** (color/brand/date) as search filters - cheap
   metadata to keep at extraction time.
3. **Smart Spaces = saved views again** (third independent sighting, with
   Obsidian Bases and Capacities smart queries: auto-sorting collections are
   table stakes for this category).
4. **The privacy manifesto** as copy direction for Axiom's own story.

## Where Axiom can differentiate

1. **Understanding, not just finding**: summarization with verified claims,
   open questions, and links into the graph vs. associative retrieval of
   saved items.
2. **Action**: captures become tasks/contacts/drafts through proposals;
   mymind deliberately stops at retrieval.
3. **Correctability**: Axiom's classification is inspectable (signals,
   confidence, accept/reject) where mymind's AI filing is a black box.
