# Market synthesis: the personal-OS / second-brain landscape (2026-10-25)

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Analyst:** overnight build session · **Status:** synthesis of the 13 dated entries in this directory; each claim traces to an entry's sources.

## The one sentence

The market is abandoning "a better place to put notes" and converging on
"an assistant that does the work" - and every player who moved lost
something Axiom is architected to keep.

## Signal 1: The agentic pivot is real, and it is an exodus

Three independent pivots away from note-organization as the product, all
verified this cycle:

- **Tana**: from PKM outliner to agentic meeting OS (the outliner survives,
  maintained, at outliner.tana.inc).
- **Mem**: from AI notes to "AI chief of staff" (proactive briefings,
  follow-ups, scheduling).
- **Amie**: from the "joyful" calendar to botless AI meeting intelligence
  with post-meeting automation and an MCP server.

Read together with incumbents: Notion ships agents that do multi-step work;
NotebookLM (Google) keeps adding generation surfaces (Cinematic Video
Overviews, flashcards, reports). Nobody with traction is investing in
manual organization UX. **The capture -> verify -> propose -> act thesis is
the direction the whole market is walking; Axiom's difference is walking it
with receipts.**

## Signal 3: Table stakes that emerged (independent sightings)

- **Saved views / auto-sorting collections**: Obsidian Bases, Capacities
  smart queries, mymind Smart Spaces. Three sightings = expected.
- **Model choice / BYOK**: Reflect BYOK, Recall model choice, Notion's
  model routing. Axiom's provider registry is built for this.
- **Proactive surfacing with restraint**: Mem, Saner.AI, Tana all sell
  "surfaces what needs attention." The ambient-Eve direction (ASH-313) is
  validated; the bar is context + a clear next step, never noise.
- **The intake triage loop**: Akiflow's universal inbox, Saner.AI's
  inbox-triage, Axiom's ASH-272 - intake -> AI suggests -> human confirms.
  Axiom's version is the only one with provenance (model, prompt version,
  confidence, rationale).
- **Chat-ops over one's own data** at the right altitude: Aki's "block an
  hour," Amie's MCP, NotebookLM's grounded chat. Alfred's verbs over the
  daybook/tasks/calendar are the same shape.

## Signal 4: The ritual is a moat nobody automated

Sunsama's premium price rests on two ceremonies - the morning plan and the
evening shutdown (ASH-305's shape). Everyone else sells either storage or
dispatch. **A guided ritual whose labor the assistant does for you** (draft
the plan from tasks + calendar + triage; close the day with a real summary)
is unoccupied: Sunsama has the ritual without the AI, the assistants have
the AI without the ritual.

## What this says to build (and not build)

1. **Keep the receipts as the headline.** Every competitor asks for trust;
   Axiom shows provenance. The verified-summary pipeline, the proposal
   pattern with rationale, and the registry/quota gates are the brand.
2. **Extend proposals to edits and merges** (Tana's merge-proposals-with-
   diffs is the strongest single idea this cycle): the proposal pattern
   generalizes past creation.
3. **Ritual surfaces over raw surfaces**: the daybook should read as a
   morning plan and an evening shutdown, not a list.
4. **Do not chase meeting bots or MCP distribution yet.** Both are real
   (Amie proves both), both are decisions for the owner: recording consent
   ergonomics and the MCP authz boundary are product calls, not
   implementation details.
5. **Niche-first GTM**: Saner.AI's "for ADHD" outsells "for everyone" in
   testimonials across the set. Axiom's wedge (people whose work is reading
   + decisions) is the equivalent.
