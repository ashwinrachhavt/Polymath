# Market research: Saner.AI

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Analyst:** overnight build session · **Status:** current as of 2026-10-25 sources
**Category:** AI personal assistant for notes/tasks/email (ADHD-positioned).

## What it is

A personal-assistant product ("Skai") aimed explicitly at people with ADHD:
one chat that reads your email, calendar, notes, and files, turns emails and
brain dumps into tasks, plans your day, and - its most praised feature -
**proactively surfaces what needs attention** without being asked. A third
sighting (with Tana and Mem) of the same market move: assistant over
organization. [S1]

## Current feature set (verified 2026-10-25)

- **Chat-to-plan**: brain dump in, structured plan/tasks out; one-click
  prioritize + schedule onto a timeline view. [S1]
- **Email -> tasks**; inbox triage where "for each new item, AI suggests
  tasks by understanding and connecting everything." [S1]
- **Proactive assistant**: surfaces follow-ups, priorities, and forgotten
  items without prompting (the testimonial centerpiece). [S1]
- **Everything becomes notes**: emails, files, chats stored as note objects;
  save chat outputs back into notes mid-conversation ("feedback loop"). [S1]
- **Voice-to-text capture**, folder system, timeline/calendar view. [S1]

## Business model

Freemium with a paid standard tier (user testimonial references "the paid
standard version"; exact pricing not re-verified tonight). [S1]

## Strengths

- **Inbox-zero-for-everything as the core loop**: one intake, AI sorts and
  suggests, human confirms - the exact shape of Axiom's capture triage, which
  validates ASH-272's product thesis independently.
- A sharp underserved-audience wedge (ADHD) instead of "everyone who takes
  notes."
- The chat-to-notes feedback loop keeps knowledge accreting without mode
  switches.

## Weaknesses

- **Suggestion quality is the whole product** and is unverifiable from
  outside; no visible provenance or confidence on its suggestions.
- Everything-in-one-cloud with deep email/calendar access is a big trust ask
  for a small company.
- No structured knowledge layer (no claims, links, or graph): notes are
  containers for the assistant to read.

## What Axiom can assimilate

1. **The triage loop shape, confirmed by a competitor's users**: intake ->
   AI suggests tasks from each item -> human confirms. Axiom's capture
   proposals do this with provenance (model, prompt version, confidence,
   rationale) that Saner does not show. Ship the loop; keep the receipts.
2. **Save-from-chat back into the knowledge base** mid-conversation - a
   small, high-frequency interaction worth copying onto the Alfred surface.
3. **Proactive surfacing with restraint** - third sighting (Mem, Tana): the
   ambient-Eve direction call (ASH-313) has strong market validation. The
   bar: proactive only with context + a clear next step.
4. **Niche-first positioning** as GTM learning: "for ADHD" beats "for
   everyone" in this category's testimonials.

## Where Axiom can differentiate

1. **Provenance and confidence on every suggestion** vs. opaque assistant
   suggestions.
2. **Structured knowledge under the assistant** (verified zettels, evidence
   rows, typed domains) vs. note blobs the AI reads.
3. **Owner-controlled automation**: proposals + registry gates vs. deep
   always-on inbox access.
